NeuroClips

A personal tool that writes, narrates and uploads short videos to its owner’s own YouTube channel.

What it does

NeuroClips is a self-hosted script. It drafts a short script, narrates it with a text-to-speech voice, assembles the result into a vertical video with captions, and uploads it to a single YouTube channel — the channel belonging to the person running it.

Who it is for

One person: its operator. It is not a service, it has no users to sign up, no accounts, and no interface other than a command line on the owner’s own computer.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the operator’s YouTube channel
  • Read access to that channel’s own view and revenue statistics, so the operator can see how the videos performed

It reads no one else’s data and has no access to any other account.
